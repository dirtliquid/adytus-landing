How it works

Private by design,
not by promise.

Three layers make sure your company data never leaves your control — even we can't see it.

1

Your team connects their AI

Each employee brings their own API key for ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. No shared credentials, no account pooling.

2

Prompts are encrypted before sending

Every message is encrypted on the client before it reaches any server. Even Adytus infrastructure never sees plaintext prompts.

3

Zero retention, zero training

No conversation history is stored. Your data is never used to train models. Admin access is architecturally impossible.

Privacy architecture

Five guarantees.
Not five promises.

These are architectural constraints — not policies that can be changed with a config flag.

Client-side encryption

Prompts are encrypted in the browser before any network request. The server receives ciphertext only.

Zero retention

No conversations are stored on our servers. When the session ends, the data is gone.

Admin-blind

Company admins can see usage metadata but never conversation content. It's architecturally impossible.

Private infrastructure

Hosted on HuggingFace private spaces. Not shared multi-tenant infrastructure.

Anonymous payment

Pay with crypto. No identity required. Your organization's relationship with Adytus stays private.

Why the name

The most sacred
room in the temple.

In ancient Greek architecture, the adyton was the innermost sanctuary — a room so private that only the most trusted priests were permitted to enter.

We chose this name because that's what we're building: a private space where your company's most sensitive thinking can happen freely, without fear of exposure.
