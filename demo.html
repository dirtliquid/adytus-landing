Under the hood

Zero-knowledge encryption

Every prompt is encrypted client-side before it touches our servers. We store ciphertext. The key lives with you.

01
You type a prompt
Your prompt exists as plain text only inside your browser tab. It never travels unprotected.
02
Browser encrypts it
adytus encrypts the prompt in-browser with your key before sending anything. We receive gibberish.
03
AI gets the real thing
Your encrypted prompt is relayed directly to the AI provider via your API key. The response comes back encrypted to you.
